      ❓ How to play

      🆕 What's new in v1.0
      • 🎃 Halloween event until Nov 2: spooky foods, a new mutation, two recipes and something knocking...
      • 📅 Daily challenges: three new goals every day for coins. Finish all three to build a streak.
      • 👤 Profiles: everyone on this browser gets their own save, with an optional password.
      • 🪙 Shop search: find what you can sell, and see why some things can't be sold.
      • ✌️ Double mutations: once in a while, food mutates twice.
      • 🍀 Easier top tiers: legendary, mythic, divine and eternal foods all show up more often.
      • 🎲 Roll for food. Each food has odds like "1 in 80". Luck makes the rare ones show up more.
      • 🍽️ Eat food from your Inventory for buffs: more luck, faster rolls. The more buffs you stack, the less each extra one adds. ⭐ keeps a food safe, 🍴 picks it for auto eat, and "Eat all" eats a whole tier at once.
      • 🧬 Mutations can happen to any food. They make it stronger and rarer. Once in a while, food mutates twice.
      • 🍲 Cook in the Kitchen by mixing foods in the pot. Recipe scraps hint at what goes together.
      • 🪙 Sell food in the Shop. Bigger food sells for more. Spend coins on scraps, power-ups and upgrades.
      • 📅 Daily challenges change every day at midnight. Finish all three for a bonus that grows with your streak.
      • 🏆 Achievements pay coins. Secrets are hidden all over the game, and their hints are vague on purpose.
      • ⚙️ Settings has sound, cutscenes and a save backup.

      💾 Back up your save

      Your progress only lives in this browser. If its data gets cleared, it's gone. Export a backup code in Settings and keep it somewhere safe, like a note or a message to yourself.

      🎲 Auto roll unlocked!

      You rolled 15 times. Now the game can roll for you.

      Tick "Auto roll" under the Roll button. Pair it with Auto eat for luck while you wait.

      🍽️ Eat food for luck!

      Your luck only goes up when you eat. Without it, the rare foods stay very rare.

      Eat food from your Inventory, or let Auto eat do it for you. "Common to Rare" keeps your best finds safe.
